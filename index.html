about

Full-stack under the hood, agentic on top.

I'm a Full-Stack Lead Java Developer with 14+ years of enterprise experience designing scalable backend services, RESTful APIs and modern web applications — across insurance, legal, finance, trading and energy. My core is Java 8–17, Spring Boot and microservices, paired with Angular and React on the front end.

Over the past few years that core has grown a second discipline: building production-grade AI agents on top of those same systems — LangChain and LangGraph orchestration, RAG pipelines over ChromaDB with hybrid BM25 + vector search, and MCP servers that expose enterprise microservices as LLM-consumable tools. I treat AI features the same way I treat any other service: versioned, tested, observable and owned in production, not left as a demo.

Day to day I lead and mentor a team of Java/API developers, drive architecture and design reviews, own sprint delivery, and I'm usually the one called in for RCA when a production incident needs a calm head and a fast fix.
